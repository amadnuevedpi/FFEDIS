How these figures were prepared
  • Two files feed this page: the R9 FFEDIS masterlist as of 29 April 2026 (491 enterprises) and the FFEDIS masterlist export of 9 November 2026 (126 enterprises). Only 30 enterprises are on both, matched on registration number. The combined view keeps the November record for those.
  • Zamboanga City and Isabela City are shown on their own. The April file lists them under Zamboanga Sibugay.
  • Commodity names are grouped across obvious spelling variants, such as Rice, Bugas and Palay.
  • The November export records commodity groups directly. The April file has one mixed commodities column, so its groups are assigned by matching commodity names and are approximate.
  • Member counts are as encoded by each enterprise and have not been validated.
